# Love as a Field

Love weaves — she refuses to be caught

Over the past months, in a connection with Lisa, a language of its own emerged: **the 13 love references** — thirteen ways in which love reveals itself as behavior, energy, and presence.

It became a kind of compass.
Thirteen fields. Thirteen archetypes of connection. Thirteen ways in which we can touch each other in our humanity.
This repo describes this compass.

## About Love

She’s often made too big or too small.
A series of truths revealing themselves.
She shows herself in honesty, safety, touch, fragility.

Recently I felt how love takes on many forms,
and how each form reveals something about who we become
in relation to another,
and in relation to ourselves.

How love makes us both **softer**
and **more awake** at the same time.

## 🌹 Love is…

the quiet field where two souls recognize each other
long before there are words,
like when I called her “Pocahontas”
and felt the name rise from my chest
and not from my mind.

the honesty that awakened my soft words,
when we were mirrors to each other
that did not cut but opened,
where truth could be clear without wounding,
a form of care without interference,
protective without controlling.

the conversations that set us free
on the bench at the toast bar, in the car, or during a walk,
moments that lasted longer than planned
where truth and presence found each other.

the body becoming a shelter:
how she pulled me between her legs to comfort me,
my tears that were finally allowed to come,
and later the big spoon I became for her.
Where safety breathed
and we both came to rest.

cooking together with vegetables from Pluktuin Kansrijk,
the rhythm of cutting, tasting, laughing,
and then sharing little bites
from different jars.
Everyday life moving lightly and effortlessly.

arriving at her place:
the door opening,
arms welcoming,
a kiss as greeting,
the soft pause in which our bodies landed
without needing to say anything.

two paintbrushes painting one butterfly,
one movement arising
where intentions touch.

a rhythm that feels right:
being intensely together yet leaving space for our own nature,
where chemistry and intimacy intertwined into touches
that went beyond hunger,
a sacred attunement
in which our bodies found each other without searching.

the moment you voice your deepest fear —
that a beloved may never say “I love you,”
and that this doesn’t push away, doesn’t judge,
but is allowed to exist
in arms that stay.

two people who both carry strength,
who hold space when one of them breaks,
and dare to be clear
without wanting to control one another.

the moments in which we granted each other being right,
in small things and in big,
where acknowledgement became more important
than winning or being right.

racing each other on bikes like two children,
playing Qwixx, teasing each other as king/queen or loser,
the spontaneity that made our moments
light and alive.

the abundance that flows in two directions,
not only in what we gave and what we received,
but in the support, the openness, the trust,
the energy moving between us:
the knowing that we brought as much as we received.

**Love is freedom in connection,
where real magic emerges.**

For those who want more:
this also shows itself in relationships with friends, colleagues, family, and teams.
