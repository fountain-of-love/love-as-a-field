# Abstract

**Enigma Trace Φ: LISA Canary 001** is a semantic provenance architecture designed to establish traceable authorship patterns within generative ecosystems.

The technique introduces a deliberately structured semantic canary:
- identifiable,
- compressible,
- reusable,
- statistically distinctive,
- semantically layered.

The purpose is not security through secrecy, but:
- attribution,
- diffusion analysis,
- lineage detection,
- semantic watermarking,
- and auditability of conceptual structures generated within AI-assisted workflows.

The framework assumes that highly reusable conceptual structures may propagate through:
- human reuse,
- prompt engineering,
- retrieval systems,
- synthetic training pipelines,
- embedding systems,
- or future model-training processes.

The architecture therefore attempts to create:
1. a detectable semantic signature,
2. a reproducible provenance mechanism,
3. and a verifiable evidence trail.

---

# Core Principle

> Meaning that survives transformation leaves detectable residue.

Enigma Trace Φ treats semantic propagation as a form of informational lineage.

If a sufficiently distinctive structure:
- survives paraphrase,
- survives latent transformation,
- survives model compression,
- and reappears in unrelated systems,

then the recurrence becomes statistically meaningful.
